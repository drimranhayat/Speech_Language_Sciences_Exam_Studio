## Reading guide

- [Catherine Anderson, Essentials of Linguistics (2018), McMaster University](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics/): Open textbook used for foundations, sounds, morphology, syntax and meaning.
- [Essentials of Linguistics, 2nd edition (Cégep Limoilou adaptation)](https://pressbooks.pub/essentialsoflinguistics2cl/): Open textbook reference for expanded lexical semantics, pragmatics and contemporary distinctions.
- [George Yule, The Study of Language, Cambridge University Press](https://www.cambridge.org/features/linguistics/yule/contents.htm): Course alignment and recommended revision reading. The publisher contents were checked; no unverified page citations are supplied.
- [International Phonetic Association: official IPA chart](https://www.internationalphoneticassociation.org/content/ipa-chart): Reference for standard articulatory categories and IPA notation.
- [Peter Ladefoged & Keith Johnson, A Course in Phonetics](https://linguistics.berkeley.edu/acip/): Specialist reading for Lecture 3. University-hosted companion materials support articulation, transcription and phonetic practice.
- [Rochelle Lieber, Introducing Morphology](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/introducing-morphology/301B4B3CEB4045756E2112603B719426): Specialist reading for Lecture 4: morphemes, word formation and morphological analysis. Publisher description and available chapter summary checked.
- [Rodney Huddleston, Geoffrey K. Pullum & Brett Reynolds, A Student’s Introduction to English Grammar, 2nd edition](https://assets.cambridge.org/97813165/14641/frontmatter/9781316514641_frontmatter.pdf): Reading for Lectures 4–6: word classes, phrases, clauses and category/function distinctions. Publisher frontmatter identifies this modern descriptive grammar; chapter conventions may differ from traditional handout labels.
- [Andrew Radford et al., Linguistics: An Introduction, 2nd edition](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/linguistics/9AEE7EDA6697286AFE1B2399DFDD9020): Broader reading for linguistic structure and analysis. Publisher listing checked; useful alongside the shorter general introductions.
- [John I. Saeed, Semantics, 5th edition](https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/humanities-social-sciences/semantics-978-1-119-70985-5): Specialist reading for Lectures 7–8: lexical relations, reference, semantic roles, truth, context and inference. Publisher contents and description checked.
- [Stephen C. Levinson, Pragmatics](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/pragmatics/6D0011901AE9E92CBC1F5F21D7C598C3): More demanding reference for Lecture 8: deixis, implicature, presupposition and speech acts. Publisher contents and description checked. Use selected sections after the introductory material.
